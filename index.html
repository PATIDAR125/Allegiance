01 — Our Philosophy

Education is not simply about what students know. It is about who they become.

The Allegiance Charter — Principle No. 1

02 — The Allegiance Approach

Learning should extend beyond the classroom.

At Allegiance Academy, the timetable is only the beginning. Rigorous academics sit alongside studios, stages, workshops and playing fields — places where creativity is practised daily, leadership is earned through service, and character is shaped by the small, repeated choices our students make every day.

From their first year, every child belongs to a house, a club and a community — three threads that turn education into belonging.

03 — Our Houses

Four Houses. One Community.

The House of Inquiry. Named for the physicist C.V. Raman, Raman House champions scientific temper — curiosity tested by experiment, and ideas defended with courage.

Meet Raman House

The House of Valour. Inspired by Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose, Shubhas House stands for discipline, daring and an unshakeable will to lead from the front.

Meet Shubhas House

The House of Service. Carrying Gandhi's belief in truth and quiet strength, Gandhi House leads through empathy, sustainability and community action.

Meet Gandhi House

The House of Imagination. In Tagore's spirit, this house gives shape to ideas — through verse, canvas, stage and song.

Meet Tagore House
