04 — Visit Us

Come see an ordinary day.

Visiting Hours

Campus visits run on working days, 09:00 – 14:00, by appointment. Weekend visits can be arranged around school events — just ask.

Directions

The campus sits off Education City Road, ten minutes from the city centre. Visitor parking is inside the main gate; the admissions office is the first building past the quadrangle.

What to Expect

A coordinator walks you through the school during lessons — studios open, field in use — with time to meet the section head for your child's grade. No dress code, no preparation; bring your questions.
