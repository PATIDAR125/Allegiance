01 — Why Allegiance

Four reasons families choose us.

Not slogans — the things a school must get right every single day.

01

Holistic Learning

Academics sit inside a larger timetable — studios, laboratories, fields and stages — so a strong report card is the floor, never the ceiling.

02

Student Development

Every child is known by name and by need. Mentors track progress in small cohorts, and support arrives before struggle becomes silence.

03

Art, music, drama and making are taught as disciplines, not diversions — with real exhibitions, real productions and real audiences.

03 — Important Information

The details, in plain words.

  • Admission is open for Grades I–XII, subject to seats being available in the receiving class. The age criterion for each grade follows the education-board norm for the admission year, and a small relaxation may be discussed with the section head during the visit.

  • Copies are sufficient at the application stage; originals are verified only after an offer is made.

    • Birth certificate of the student
    • Previous school report card and transfer certificate, where applicable
    • Address proof of the parent or guardian
    • Two passport photographs of the student

  • Enquiries are accepted through the year. The formal application window for each session typically opens in autumn and closes when seats are filled — earlier applicants are scheduled for visits first. Exact dates are announced on the news page and by email to every enquiry on file.

  • Visits are held on working days so you see an ordinary day, not a performance. A coordinator walks you through the campus, arranges time with the relevant section head, and answers questions on transport, meals and daily routines. Book through the enquiry form or the admissions office.

  • A short, standard list: birth certificate, previous report card and transfer certificate where applicable, address proof, and photographs. Copies at application; originals only after an offer. The full checklist appears in the Important Information section above.
