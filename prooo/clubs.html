01 — Club Directory

Eight ways to belong.

Hover a row to see where it happens. Tap on touch. Every club meets weekly and is run, in large part, by students.

  • Debate, poetry, quizzes and the school magazine — for students who think best in sentences and argue best in paragraphs.

  • Experiments beyond the syllabus, astronomy nights on the terrace and the annual innovation fair, where prototypes meet their first critics.

  • Studio practice, murals and two exhibitions a year — drawing the campus, then redrawing it the way it feels.

  • Choir, classical ensemble and the student production crew that scores every assembly, festival and graduation.

  • Team training, house fixtures and a fitness culture that runs through every season — competitive for some, conditioning for all.

  • Stagecraft, scriptwriting and two full productions a year — with room in the wings for anyone who prefers the spotlight indirect.

  • Robotics, coding and the maker lab — building things that beep, move, break, and eventually matter.

  • Outreach drives, sustainability projects and long-running partnerships with local causes — service treated as a habit, not a week.

Featured Activity

The Annual Production.

Every season, auditions open for the year's biggest student production — and the whole school steps in. Sixty or more students on stage and behind it: set builders, lighting crew, costume, sound, stage management. Weeks of rehearsal end in three nights the corridors talk about until the next one.
