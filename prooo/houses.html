The Houses

Four Houses. One Spirit.

Competition creates energy. Community creates belonging.

Scroll

01 / Raman · The House of Inquiry

Raman

Named for the physicist C.V. Raman, Raman House treats curiosity as a discipline. Its corridors argue, test and measure — and its members are the first to admit when an experiment fails, because that is where the next one starts.

House spirit

“Question everything.”

02 / Shubhas · The House of Valour

Shubhas

Inspired by Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose, Shubhas House stands for discipline, daring and an unshakeable will to lead from the front. On the field, on stage and in assembly, Shubhas arrives first and leaves last.

House spirit

“Lead from the front.”

03 / Gandhi · The House of Service

Gandhi

Carrying Gandhi's belief in truth and quiet strength, Gandhi House leads through empathy, sustainability and service. Its members plant more than they post — and measure a good year by what the community around the school gained.

House spirit

“Serve before you speak.”

04 / Tagore · The House of Imagination

Tagore

In Rabindranath Tagore's spirit, Tagore House gives shape to ideas — through verse, canvas, stage and song. It is the house most likely to be rehearsing at dusk and exhibiting by morning.

House spirit

“Make it beautiful.”
